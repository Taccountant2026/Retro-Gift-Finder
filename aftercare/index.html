BEFORE CONTACTING SUPPORT

What information should I include?

Your order number, console model, when the issue began, what appears on screen, and the checks you have already tried. Photos or a short video can help where appropriate.

Should I send the console back immediately?

No. Contact RetroReplay UK first and wait for return instructions. This helps ensure the correct address, packaging and tracked service are used.

Where are the warranty and returns terms?

Read the current terms on the RetroReplay UK website because they apply to the individual order and remain the authoritative version.
